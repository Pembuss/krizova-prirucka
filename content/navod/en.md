The Crisis Handbook is **a book, a planner and a map in one app**. It helps you prepare your household for a power cut, flood, fire, epidemic, chemical leak or armed conflict. When a crisis comes, it guides you step by step. It is free, with no ads and no sign-up, and it works offline too.

::: warn
**If a life is in danger, always call 112 first.** The app does not replace instructions from your municipality, police, fire brigade or crisis staff. Follow radio, TV and emergency alerts.
:::

## What the app does

- **Crisis now:** immediate steps for 15 types of crisis, one step per screen.
- **Handbook:** the whole book with 36 chapters inside the app, with search and “for you” choices. You can also download it as a printable PDF and as an EPUB for e-readers.
- **Map:** shelters, hospitals, water and other places nearby. The map works offline and also plans escape routes.
- **Prepare:** a readiness plan, household profile, supplies and shopping list, backup power, emergency bags and a family plan.
- **Print:** family plan, wallet cards, action cards and shopping lists on A4 paper.
- **Five languages:** Slovak, Czech, English, Polish and Hungarian.

Your data (household, supplies, plan) stays **only on your device**. It is never sent anywhere. When you search for an address or a route, only the search text and coordinates go to public map services.

## Installing and working offline

The app runs in your web browser: just open its web address and it starts straight away. The first time you open it on the web, it offers two options:

- **Install on my phone** (recommended). On Android, and on a computer with Chrome or Edge, an install dialog opens and you just confirm it. On iPhone and in other browsers, the app shows step-by-step instructions for your device.
- **Continue in the browser.** The app opens straight away. You can still install it later: **More → Help → Install on my phone**, in Settings, or from the reminder on the Home screen.

Once installed, the app starts full screen from the icon on your home screen, without this choice.

::: shots
![First visit on the web](shot:install)

![Instructions for iPhone](shot:install-ios)

To install it manually:

- **Android (Chrome):** tap **⋮** in the top right and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** tap the **Share** icon (a square with an arrow) and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Computer (Chrome, Edge):** click the install icon in the address bar, or open **Settings → Install app** in the app.

The first time you open the app, it saves itself on your device together with the book, the action steps, the lists and an overview map of Europe. The detailed map is saved gradually as you look at places. **Settings → Offline mode** shows whether everything is saved. The button **Save the book (PDF, EPUB) and all languages offline** also saves the downloadable files.

::: tip
**Copy for a computer or USB stick:** in Settings choose **Download offline package**. Unzip the file and open **OTVOR-PRIRUCKU.html**. This copy works with no internet and no installation, even on someone else's computer.
:::

::: note
**iPhone:** add the app to your Home Screen. If you only use it in Safari, Safari may delete saved data after a few days without use. You can download a backup in Settings.
:::

## First start

The first time you open the app, a short welcome guide takes you through four steps:

1. **Language:** pick one of five languages. The Polish and Hungarian versions are marked as unverified until a native speaker has reviewed them.
2. **Look:** pick one of two looks, Earth tabs or Tiles, and the text size (see below).
3. **Household:** say how many of you there are (adults, children, seniors, pets) and whether you live in a flat or a house. The app uses this to calculate supplies and tailor the book.
4. **Done:** start using the app, or take the **app tour**.

## Crisis now

When something is happening, tap **Crisis** in the bottom menu or the big button on the Home screen.

1. **Choose the situation**, for example Power outage (blackout) or Flood and flash flood. The **Call 112** button is always at the top.
2. The app shows **one step per screen**. When you have done it, tap **Done** and continue with **Next step**. The bar at the top shows how many steps are left.
3. To see all the steps at once, switch to **Full list**. **Step by step** takes you back to one step at a time.
4. At the end you will see which steps are still unticked, plus links to **shelters on the map**, **escape routes** and **the detailed chapter in the book**.

## Handbook (the book)

The **Handbook** section holds the whole book. Its 36 chapters cover preparation, each type of crisis, first aid, mental health, vulnerable people and self-reliance. Lists, templates and a glossary are at the end.

- **Search the handbook:** type a word, for example *water*, *siren* or *iodine tablets*. Tap a result to jump straight to that place in the chapter.
- **Start reading** opens the book at the beginning. The **Previous** and **Next** buttons at the end of each chapter turn the pages. **Contents** opens the chapter's table of contents.
- **Signpost: what to read** suggests an order of chapters for a crisis or for your situation.
- **Download:** a printable PDF (A4) and an EPUB for e-readers, in every language. An e-ink reader lasts weeks without charging, which helps during a power cut.
- **Stories – play:** interactive scenarios where you live through a crisis as a story and make your own decisions.

### Gamebook and “for you” choices

The book works like a gamebook: at the end of sections it offers **choices** of where to go next. Under **Tailor the book to your household**, mark what applies to you, such as children, seniors, a flat, no car or pets. The book then highlights the choices **for you**, and the end of each chapter shows **Recommended for you next**. If you have filled in the household profile, just tap **Use my app profile**.

When you jump to another chapter, a **Back to your choice** button appears at the bottom of the screen and takes you back to where you left off. Chapters you have read are marked, so you know where you stopped.

## Map and escape routes

The **Map** shows civil protection shelters, hospitals, pharmacies, water sources, fire stations and other important places.

- Type an **address or town** in the box at the top, or tap **My location** (GPS).
- To set your home, click on the map and choose **Set as home**. You can drag the home marker to the exact spot.
- Choose a **Radius** and tap **Load places nearby**.
- Use **Layers** to turn types of places on or off.

![Map](shot:map)

The offline map covers Slovakia in detail, main roads in neighbouring countries and an overview of all of Europe. When you are online, the map adds detail from OpenStreetMap. Shelters appear on the map where Slovak towns have published them. For the full overview, see **More → Shelters in Slovakia**. Information about shelters in other countries is under **European countries**.

**Escape routes** (under More) suggest several routes to your evacuation destinations, such as family, a cottage or a shelter, by car, on foot or by bike. Each route lists its risks: bridges, rivers, tunnels and nearby nuclear power plants. Within the offline map area, routes are calculated even without internet. You can print a route card.

## Preparing your household

The **More** section has everything you need to prepare:

| Section | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **My plan** | preparation steps in order of importance, and the risk profile of your household and location |
| **Household profile** | location, household members, pets, housing and energy, shelter, mobility and evacuation destinations, supplies and budget (8 short steps, about 5 minutes) |
| **Supplies** | how much water, food and medicine you need for 3 to 30 days, plus a shopping list, step-by-step buying and stock rotation |
| **Power** | how much backup power you need in a power cut, by scenario and duration |
| **Bags and lists** | an emergency bag for each household member |
| **Family plan** | meeting points, a contact outside the area, communication, children and school, main shut-off valves |
| **Action cards** | immediate steps for each crisis, ready to print |
| **Checklists** | lists to tick off |
| **European countries** | emergency numbers, warning signals and shelters across Europe |

## Print

Under **More → Print**, choose what to print: the risk profile and steps, the family emergency plan, wallet cards, “I need help” cards, action cards, the shopping list, stock rotation, bags and power.

Everything prints on A4, is readable in black and white, and has boxes to tick. Keep the printed plan with your documents and put one copy in your emergency bag. In the print dialog you can also choose **Save as PDF**.

## Settings and your data

In **Settings** (More → Settings) you can change:

- the **look** and **text size**,
- the **language**,
- how **Crisis now** opens (step by step or as a full list),
- **Offline mode**: status, saving the books offline, the offline ZIP package and installation.

Under **My data**:

- **Download backup:** saves your profile, plan, supplies and routes to a JSON file.
- **Download password-encrypted backup:** the same, but the file cannot be opened without the password. Remember the password, because it cannot be recovered.
- **Restore from backup:** loads a backup, for example on a new phone or on a family member's phone.
- **Delete all data from this device.**

On a phone, tapping **Download backup** opens a menu to choose where to save it (Files, Google Drive, iCloud, email, a message). The backup also includes the chapters you have read and your ticks. If you have not backed up for a long time, the app reminds you on the Home screen.

**App update:** the app checks for a new version by itself when you open it. When one is ready, an **Update** bar appears. Your data is kept. To check by hand, go to **Settings → App update → Check for updates**. If the app behaves oddly, use **Repair the app**, which downloads its files again.

::: tip

## When something does not work

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| The app does not show the new version | Close it and open it again. On a computer, reload the page (F5). You can see the version in Settings. |
| My data is gone | Data is stored in the browser. Clearing it or using a private window removes it. Restore it from a backup. |
| The map has no detail | Outside Slovakia, the offline map is an overview only. Go online and look at the area, and it will be saved. |
| A PDF does not download | Check your connection. To use the files offline, save them in advance in Settings. |
| The text is too small | Settings → Text size. |
| Location (GPS) does not work | Allow the browser to access your location. Otherwise, search for the address or click on the map. |

**Data as of:** the book, the action steps and the numbers were checked in October 2026. Official instructions can change. In a crisis, the current instructions of your municipality, police, fire brigade and crisis staff always come first.
