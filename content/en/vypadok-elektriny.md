# Power outage (blackout) {#vypadok-elektriny}

Without electricity almost everything stops: lights, the fridge, the cooker, heating, water pumps in apartment buildings, the mobile network and internet (once the backup batteries of the masts run flat), card terminals, cash machines, petrol stations, traffic lights and lifts. A short outage is an inconvenience. **A widespread outage lasting more than 24 hours** – a blackout – is a crisis that affects all other services. Recent examples include Spain and Portugal (April 2025) and Czechia (July 2025).

## What you need to know {#vypadok-elektriny-vediet}

**What happens over time (indicative):**

| Time | What stops working |
|---|---|
| Immediately | lights, electrical appliances, lifts, traffic lights, electric gates, Wi-Fi internet, most heating |
| Within 1–4 hours | backup batteries of mobile masts run down – the mobile network weakens and fails; water pumps in apartment buildings may stop; the fridge warms up |
| Within 24 hours | petrol stations and shops without backup power close, card payments fail, food in the fridge spoils, homes cool down, hospitals and essential services run on generators |
| Several days | problems with water supply, sewage, fuel, medicines and food; growing risk of CO poisoning and fires from improvised heating |

- **Your electricity distributor** has a fault line and an outage map. Major outages are announced by the media and municipalities.
- **A fridge** keeps food safe for about **4 hours** if you keep it closed; **a full freezer about 48 hours**, a half-full one about 24 hours.
- **A phone** in power-saving mode with reduced brightness lasts much longer. A 20,000 mAh power bank charges an ordinary smartphone about 3 times.

## During the outage {#vypadok-elektriny-pocas}

### The first hour {#vypadok-elektriny-hodina}

- Find out whether the outage affects only you (circuit breaker, neighbours) or is widespread (radio).
- Switch off appliances that could be damaged or cause a fire when power returns (cooker, iron, heaters), and leave one light switched on.
- In an apartment building, **fill containers with water** while it is still running.
- Check whether anyone is stuck in a **lift** – if so, call 112.
- Turn on the battery radio.

### The first day {#vypadok-elektriny-den}

- Eat food from the fridge first, then from the freezer, and only then your long-life supplies.
- **Save batteries**: one light for the whole room (a lantern, or a head torch facing into a bottle of water spreads the light), phone in power-saving mode, a laptop as a charging source only in an emergency.
- **A car** can charge phones – run the engine only outdoors, never in a garage.
- Cooking without electricity: only **outdoors or with good ventilation** ([Self-reliance](#sebestacnost-varenie)).
- Check on your neighbours, especially older people and people on medical devices.

### A longer outage {#vypadok-elektriny-dlhsi}

- Keep warm in one room ([Frost](#mrazy-vypadok)).
- Hygiene with minimal water; an emergency toilet if flushing does not work ([Hygiene](#hygiena-psychika)).
- Follow municipal information about **information and help points**, water distribution and warming centres.
- Do not drive unless necessary – traffic lights are out and petrol stations may be closed.

::: warning
Run a **generator** only **outdoors**, at least 6 metres from windows, doors and vents – never in a garage, cellar or on a balcony. Plug appliances directly into the generator using an extension lead. **Never connect a generator to a wall socket** in the house – backfeed can kill electricians repairing the lines and damage the installation. Connecting to the house wiring requires a transfer switch installed by an electrician.
:::

## People who depend on electricity {#vypadok-elektriny-pristroje}

If someone in the household uses an **oxygen concentrator, CPAP, dialysis, suction device, electric wheelchair or medicines that need refrigeration**:

- Agree an **outage plan** with the doctor and the device supplier (backup batteries, spare oxygen cylinders, where to go).
- Arrange a **backup power source** (a portable power station with enough capacity, a generator) and calculate how long it will last – the app does this for each type of device.
- Inform your **electricity distributor and municipality** – some keep lists of vulnerable customers.
- During a long outage, move in good time to a place with electricity (relatives, hospital).
