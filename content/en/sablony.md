# Templates: family plan, contacts, wallet card {#sablony}

Print the templates, fill them in by hand with a pen and keep them in the agreed places: one copy on the fridge, one in the grab bag, one with your contact person outside the area. Cut out the cards and give one to every family member for their wallet or school bag. In the app, the templates are filled in automatically from your household data.

::: warning
The family plan contains sensitive data (addresses, health information). Keep it where it cannot fall into the wrong hands, and do not publish it.
:::
