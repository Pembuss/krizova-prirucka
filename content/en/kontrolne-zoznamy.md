# Checklists {#kontrolne-zoznamy}

All the checklists in one place, ready to print and tick off. Print them, hang them somewhere visible or put them in the grab bag. In the app the lists adapt to your household (number of people, children, pets, medicines) and remember what you already have.

::: tip
**How to use them:** print a list, put a ✓ next to every item you have, and write next to the missing ones the date by which you will get them. At the twice-yearly review, use a fresh printout.
:::
