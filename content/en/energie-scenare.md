# Power and essentials by scenario {#energie-scenare}

Different crises make different demands. In a summer power cut the problem is the fridge and water; in winter it is heat; in a shelter it is air and the toilet; during an evacuation it is the weight of your bags. For **seven typical scenarios**, this chapter shows what you need to arrange and how much of it you need.

## How we calculate energy {#energie-scenare-vypocet}

- **Phone:** an ordinary smartphone battery holds 15–20 Wh. In power-saving mode one charge lasts 1–2 days. We assume **9 Wh per phone per day**.
- **A 20,000 mAh power bank** holds about 74 Wh, of which about **48 Wh** is usable after losses – roughly 3 phone charges.
- **A head torch with 3 AAA batteries** runs about 30 hours on medium. With 3 hours of light a day, a person uses about **0.3 AAA batteries a day**.
- **A radio with 2 AA batteries** plays for about 50 hours; **an LED lantern with 3 AA batteries** lights for about 40 hours.
- **A 227 g cartridge** in a camping stove lasts about **2 hours** on a medium flame. A household needs about 15 minutes a day for cooking and heating water plus another 8 minutes per person.
- **A fridge** uses 0.5–1.5 kWh a day, **a freezer** about the same. **An electric heater** uses 1–2 kW per hour – unrealistic on batteries.
- **Medical devices:** CPAP about 30–60 W (300–500 Wh per night), an oxygen concentrator 150–400 W continuously (tens of kWh a day – a generator or oxygen cylinders are needed).

## Scenario 1: Summer power cut for 3 days {#energie-scenare-1}

| Need | Solution | Quantity for 1 person / family of 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Water | bottled water, fill containers in a tower block | 12 l / 39 l |
| Food | first from the fridge, then no-cook | 3-day supply |
| Cooling | keep the fridge closed, cool bag with ice packs | – |
| Light | head torches, lantern | 1 head torch / 4 head torches + lantern |
| Communication | radio, power banks | 1 power bank / 2–3 power banks |
| Heat | not needed; in hot weather ventilate at night | battery fan |
| Hygiene | wet wipes, sanitiser | 1 pack / 2–3 packs |

## Scenario 2: Winter power cut for 7 days {#energie-scenare-2}

| Need | Solution | Quantity for 1 person / family of 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Water | bottled + grey water (bath) | 28 l / 91 l including hygiene + grey water for the toilet |
| Food | long-life, a hot meal once a day | 7-day supply |
| Cooking | cartridge stove outdoors or by an open window | 2 cartridges / 3 cartridges |
| Heat | one room, layers, sleeping bags, hot-water bottles; a stove if you have one | sleeping bag / 4 sleeping bags, hot-water bottles |
| Light | head torches, lantern, batteries | 1 pack AAA / 2 packs AAA + 1 pack AA |
| Communication | radio, power banks, solar panel | 2 power banks / 3 power banks + solar panel |
| Safety | CO alarm, fire extinguisher | 1 / 1 |
| Hygiene | emergency toilet if there is no water | bucket, bags, absorbent |

## Scenario 3: Water outage for 7 days {#energie-scenare-3}

| Need | Solution | Quantity for 1 person / family of 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Drinking and cooking | bottled water, tankers | 21 l / 63 l |
| Hygiene | 1–2 l per person per day, wipes, sanitiser | 7–14 l / 28–56 l |
| Toilet | grey water (4–6 l per flush) or an emergency toilet | bucket + bags + absorbent |
| Dishes | disposable dishes | one pack |
| Water treatment | tablets, filter, stove for boiling | 1 pack of tablets |
| Carrying water | collapsible canisters | 1 / 2–3 canisters |

## Scenario 4: Sheltering for 3 days (radiation, air attack) {#energie-scenare-4}

| Need | Solution | Quantity for 1 person / family of 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Water | 6 l per person per day (2 drinking, 4 washing) | 18 l / 72 l |
| Food | no-cook only (no cooking in a shelter) | 3-day supply |
| Air | sealing, ventilation as instructed | sheeting, tape |
| Light | head torches, battery lantern (no candles) | 1 / 4 + lantern |
| Communication | battery radio | 1 radio + batteries |
| Hygiene | emergency toilet, bags, absorbent, wipes | bucket, 10 bags, 10 l absorbent |
| Sleep | sleeping mats, blankets, sleeping bags | for everyone |
| Other | medicines, first-aid kit, respirators, games for children | – |

## Scenario 5: Evacuation for 72 hours {#energie-scenare-5}

| Need | Solution | Quantity per person |
|---|---|---|
| Water | bottle and supply for the journey | 2–3 l + filter or tablets |
| Food | no-cook, light, high-calorie | 3 days (bars, nuts, tins, crispbread) |
| Light and communication | head torch, power bank, car charger | 1 + 1 |
| Warmth | sleeping bag or blanket, foil blanket, rain poncho | 1 set |
| Documents and money | waterproof pouch, cash | – |
| Weight | adult up to 25 kg, child according to age | – |

## Scenario 6: Household quarantine for 14 days (epidemic) {#energie-scenare-6}

| Need | Solution |
|---|---|
| Food | 14-day supply – electricity and water work, you can cook normally |
| Medicines | regular medicines for 30 days, fever medicine, oral rehydration salts, thermometer |
| Protection | FFP2 respirators (2 per person per day when in contact with the sick person), gloves, sanitiser |
| Hygiene | rubbish bags, cleaning products |
| Communication | internet, phone, an arrangement with neighbours for shopping |

## Scenario 7: Heatwave for 7 days {#energie-scenare-7}

| Need | Solution |
|---|---|
| Water | 1 litre more per person per day, mineral water and oral rehydration salts |
| Cooling | external shading, a fan (also battery-powered), wet towels, cooling pads |
| Food | light and cold; beware of spoilage without refrigeration |
| People | daily checks on older and sick neighbours |
| Health | a thermometer for the flat, know the signs of heatstroke |
