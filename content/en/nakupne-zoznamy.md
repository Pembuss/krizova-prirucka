# Food shopping lists for 3, 7, 14 and 30 days {#nakupne-zoznamy}

This chapter contains ready-made shopping lists for typical households. The lists were calculated by the same algorithm as the app, so they are directly comparable. For an exact list for your family (members' ages, diets, pets, cooking method, budget) use the app – you can print the same list from it with tick boxes.

::: note
**Assumptions:** an adult needs 2,200 kcal a day (children and older people less), 3 l of water per person per day for drinking and cooking plus 1 l for hygiene, and at least half of the food can be eaten without cooking. Prices are **indicative** (Slovak retail, October 2026, in euros) and are for budget estimates only – in the app you can overwrite them with prices from your own shop.
:::

## How to use the lists {#nakupne-zoznamy-pouzitie}

1. Choose the list that best matches your household and the period you want to prepare for. **Start with 3 days**, then expand.
2. Swap items you do not eat for similar ones from the same group (for example tuna for sardines, rice for couscous) – what matters is keeping the calories the same.
3. Buy **gradually** – a few extra items with every shop (see the budget plan below).
4. Write the date on your supplies and store them according to the **first in, first out** rule ([Rotation](#zasoby-obmena)).

## Diets and special needs {#nakupne-zoznamy-diety}

- **Vegetarians:** replace tinned meat and pâté with pulses, soy chunks, nuts and cheese.
- **Vegans:** in addition replace milk with plant-based drinks and honey and chocolate with vegan alternatives; add more peanut butter, nuts and pulses.
- **Gluten-free diet:** rice, rice cakes, instant mashed potato, sweetcorn, gluten-free oats and pasta; watch out for ready meals and soups.
- **Lactose-free:** UHT lactose-free milk or plant-based drinks, hard cheese (contains only traces of lactose).
- **Diabetes:** more pulses, nuts and wholegrain products, fewer sweets; a supply of **glucose tablets** in case of hypoglycaemia.
- **Babies:** infant formula or ready-to-feed liquid formula, water suitable for babies, baby food, nappies ([Vulnerable groups](#zranitelni-deti)).
- **Allergies:** check the ingredients of every item; keep antihistamines and an adrenaline auto-injector if you have been prescribed one.
