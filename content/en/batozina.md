# Grab bag and documents {#batozina}

If you have to leave quickly – during a flood, a fire, a chemical leak or an ordered evacuation – there will be no time to look for documents, medicines and chargers. A grab bag is **packed in advance** and stands by the door. It should last you **72 hours**, until you reach relatives or an evacuation centre.

::: urgent
**Act now – you must leave within 10 minutes**

1. **People and pets** – gather the family, take your pets.
2. **Documents and money** – ID card, passport, health insurance card, cash, payment cards.
3. **Medicines** – regular medicines, glasses, hearing aid, medical aids.
4. **Phone** – charger, cable, power bank.
5. **Bag** – if you have one ready, take it. If not: water, no-cook food, warm clothes, a blanket, a torch.
6. **Home** – turn off gas, water and electricity, close the windows, lock up.
:::

## Principles {#batozina-zasady}

- **Every household member has their own bag** – an adult's rucksack up to **25 kg**, a child's according to age (as a guide up to 10 kg). A rucksack is better than a suitcase – your hands stay free.
- **Water and food for 72 hours** – more water if you will be walking, food that needs no cooking.
- **Clothing for the season** – swap it at the twice-yearly review and check children's sizes.
- **A name tag** on every bag (name, phone, contact person).
- The bag stands **by the door or in the hall**, not in the attic.
- Keep a smaller kit **in the car** and **at work** too (comfortable shoes, water, a snack bar, power bank, medicines, a map of the way home).

## Documents {#batozina-dokumenty}

Keep the originals of important documents at home in **one folder in a waterproof cover** so that you can take them all at once. Put **copies** into the grab bag and store **scans** on an encrypted USB stick and in a secure cloud. You can also leave a copy of the family plan and contact list with your contact person outside the area.

{{checklist:documents}}

::: warning
Never write **PIN codes or passwords** next to your documents. If you need a password backup, use a password manager or a sealed envelope kept in a safe place.
:::

## Money {#batozina-peniaze}

During a power or payment-system outage, card terminals and cash machines will not work. Keep **cash in small notes and coins** in the grab bag – roughly enough for 3 days of shopping, fuel and accommodation. Split the cash between several places (bag, wallet, car).

## Review {#batozina-kontrola}

Twice a year, unpack and check the bag: expiry dates of food, water and medicines, whether the torch and radio work, whether the power bank is charged, clothing sizes and up-to-date contacts. The [regular review](#kontrolne-zoznamy) will help.
